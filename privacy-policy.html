Vertex AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Vertex AI Solutions & Automations ("Vertex AI", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, contact us, or use our WhatsApp and voice AI automation services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By accessing our website or using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly: Your name, business name, email address, phone number, business details, and any information you submit through our contact forms, demo requests, or during onboarding.

Information collected through our automation services: When you use our WhatsApp or voice automations, we may process conversational data, including messages, call transcripts, customer contact details, appointment and booking information, and related business records — solely to operate and improve your automation.

Automatically collected information: When you visit our website, we may collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and referring URLs through standard analytics and cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain and improve our WhatsApp and voice AI services;
  • Set up, configure, and train your automations;
  • Respond to inquiries and deliver the demo or support you request;
  • Send service communications, including booking confirmations and reminders;
  • Invoice you and manage our business relationship;
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

4. WhatsApp, Meta and Third-Party Services

Our WhatsApp automation services are delivered through WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates. When you or your customers use WhatsApp through our services, your data may also be processed by the operators of that messaging platform under their own terms and privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

Our automations may interact with calendar, CRM, telephony and messaging providers you choose to connect. Data passed to those providers is governed by their respective privacy and data-processing terms.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to store preferences, analyse site traffic, and understand how visitors use the site. You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are sent. However, some parts of our site may then function differently.

We may also use third-party analytics services that collect, monitor, and analyse this information to help us improve our website and services.

6. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Data is stored securely and access is limited to personnel who need it to provide our services.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. You transmit data to us at your own risk.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, to honour your requests and instructions, to maintain the legal and long-term rights, and to comply with our legal obligations.

Conversation and booking data collected through your automations is retained for the duration of your service agreement unless you request deletion earlier.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

  • Access: request a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erasure: request deletion of your personal data;
  • Restriction: restrict how we process your data;
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format; and
  • Objection / Withdraw consent: object to or withdraw consent for processing.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you are in California, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We do not "sell" your personal data in the CCPA sense.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business use and are not directed to children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website and communications may contain links to third-party websites or services that we do not operate or control. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to review their policies.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of our website or services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
